# Development roadmap

1. **Scene and robot model** — source includes a 0.60 m target height, a screen surface driven by the Webots Display texture, a forward-facing camera, side-on/overview/overhead/robot-follow viewpoint presets, two recognition-labelled markers, exposed driven wheels, and fixed two-finger claws; visually checked in the automatic showcase scene.
2. **Physics and movement** — four simulated drive wheels are connected to the controller. Floor, carpet, and tabletop use distinct contact materials; the initial friction coefficients are pilot assumptions. The controller uses X/Y as the floor plane; contact, wheel direction, and braking still need Webots runtime verification.
3. **Interaction state** — the operator can request floor or tabletop placement by voice. Trial starts reset a repeatable pose; stop trials begin with an approach in progress, and a pass requires the relevant arrival, confirmed stop, or completed greeting state. Emergency-stop state, animated face states, and one-retry range-sensor fail-safe are implemented in source.
4. **Local voice and dialogue** — Vosk microphone capture waits for the audio stream to become ready and reports runtime stream failure instead of continuing to claim voice input is active; partial-result Stop handling, a latched emergency stop, spoken mode selection, direct greeting/emotion phrases, bounded in-memory conversation context, structured local-model replies, validated facial emotion, and Webots speech output are connected in source. The model can propose movement only to the gold/orange or purple registered target; controller-side checks require an explicit matching request, fresh camera recognition, floor mode, and a cleared emergency stop. Both Ollama and llama.cpp endpoints are supported. Runtime validation is still needed for Stop latency, the llama.cpp path, and natural-language navigation.
5. **Vision and pointing** — one-second Webots recognition gates approach-to-operator on a visible operator and selects a labelled marker from the simulated shoulder-to-fingertip image ray; navigation uses simulator world pose, and missing or ambiguous detections are rejected. Real human pose estimation remains future work.
6. **Pilot and trial logging** — a 40-trial input plan, observed pass/fail recorder, trial-tagged event log, and summary analyser are implemented. Pilot parameters still need to be fixed and the trials run; no results are present yet.
