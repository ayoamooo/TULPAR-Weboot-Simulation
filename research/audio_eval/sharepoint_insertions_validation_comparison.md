## Report: validation-only classifier comparison

To compare lightweight classifiers without using the held-out test partitions for model selection, five candidate pipelines were assessed on validation data using the same 26-feature representation (the mean and standard deviation of 13 MFCCs). On the Speech Commands validation partition (10,102 clips), LinearSVC achieved 51.2% accuracy, 36.4% macro-F1 and 44.8% unweighted average recall (UAR). Logistic regression achieved 28.8% accuracy, 34.0% macro-F1 and 50.3% UAR, while HistGradientBoosting achieved 66.9%, 36.9% and 32.8%, respectively. These results show that the preferred candidate depends on the metric: HistGradientBoosting had the highest accuracy and macro-F1, whereas logistic regression had the highest UAR. On the actor-exclusive RAVDESS validation partition (240 clips from actors 17–20), RBF SVC achieved the highest validation scores among five candidates: 38.8% accuracy, 38.2% macro-F1 and 39.8% UAR. No deployment model is selected from these results. The comparison is exploratory, the validation partitions are limited, and the datasets do not represent TULPAR's full command phrases, exhibition-room acoustics, natural emotional speech, robot expression quality, or Raspberry Pi execution. These figures must not be presented as TULPAR system performance or as results from the planned integrated robot trials. The previously recorded held-out pilot scores remain those of the original LinearSVC baseline.
